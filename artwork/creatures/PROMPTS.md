# VESPER 2.1 — original creature textures

Generated on 6 September 2026 using the **built-in imagegen tool**, not the fallback API/CLI. These are original material images for the runtime character meshes. They are not screenshots, replacement character sprites, downloaded models or assets from Call of Duty.

The selected originals are preserved here as `face-source.png` and `skin-source.png`. Runtime derivatives are `public/textures/creatures/{face,skin}-{512,1024}.webp`. The originals were resized with Lanczos and encoded to WebP (quality 88 for the face, 86 for skin). No generative edits followed these prompts. Facial UV landmarks are registered in `src/creature-head.js`; eyes, dental arches, lips, throat and articulated mandible are actual geometry.

Create a flat front orthographic projection of the facial skin of a gaunt undead adult male for a gritty realistic zombie survival game. This is a texture for a 3D mesh, NOT a portrait with lighting. Entire canvas must be filled with skin color; no background, no neck, no ears, no hair, no text, no labels. Flattened rectangular projection of face, stretching skin naturally to all four edges. Centered bilaterally symmetric anatomy with subtle asymmetric healed scars and mottling. Exact landmarks in normalized image coordinates: eye socket centers at x0.30 and0.70 y0.41, nose tip x0.5 y0.55, mouth center x0.5 y0.75, chin x0.5 y0.92. Empty dark reddish-brown eye sockets without eyeballs or eye highlights, oval open DARK mouth width0.48 height0.17 centered at(0.5,0.75), no teeth or tongue (those are separate 3D meshes). Narrow pinched nose, tense brow and furrowed forehead, pronounced gaunt cheekbones, sunken cheeks, cracked dried lips. Desaturated pale taupe / gray cadaver skin, delicate pores, fine wrinkles, broken capillaries, old brown scars, flaking skin and irregular dull purple discoloration, restrained dried dark blood at mouth corners. Very detailed believable real skin surface, no large open wounds or exaggerated gore. Perfectly uniform diffuse illumination, NO cast shadows, NO specular highlights, NO perspective, NO beauty retouch, NO cartoon, NO skull skeleton, NO background. The requested rectangular facial projection will be UV mapped onto real head geometry.

## Skin albedo — exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless tileable PBR base color texture for original realistic undead game characters, square 1024x1024. A flat 30cm patch of aged cadaver skin material, filling every pixel edge to edge. NO face, NO body part, NO anatomy silhouette, NO eyes, NO mouth, NO objects, NO background. Extremely detailed realistic dry dead skin with microscopic pores, fine diagonal wrinkles and uneven creases, restrained broken purple capillaries, a few narrow healed scars and small irregular scaly patches. Muted desaturated taupe gray beige flesh, slight olive undertone, subtly mottled dull mauve and old brown discoloration. Keep the overall color balanced and readable, no large black blobs. Natural organic spatial variation at several scales rather than evenly distributed dots. No fresh open wounds, no extreme gore. Entire image is a FLAT MATERIAL ALBEDO MAP, perfectly even diffuse illumination, zero shadows, no directional light, no highlights, no vignette, no text, no borders. Horizontally and vertically seamless. This will be wrapped around animated zombie forearms, hands and necks in an actual videogame.
